Packing a Small Period Kit for School

by Example Staff | Oct 29, 2026 | Blog

Jamie Rivera, school nurse and health educator

Jamie Rivera, school nurse

A period can start at any time, including in the middle of a busy school day. Having a few supplies ready, and knowing who to ask for help, makes that moment much easier to handle.

Start with a Small Pouch

Any small bag works, such as a pencil case or a makeup pouch, and it can live in a backpack, a locker or a gym bag so it is always close by.

Things you might put inside:

  • A few pads, tampons or a cup, whichever you prefer
  • A spare pair of underwear
  • Wipes and a small hand sanitizer

Check the pouch every few weeks, and refill anything you used, so it is ready the next time you need it, or when a friend needs a hand.

Asking for a Break

It is always fine to tell a teacher, “I need to use the restroom for a health reason.” You never have to explain more than that, and you can read more tips here.

If you run out of supplies, the school nurse, a counselor or a trusted teacher can usually help, and many schools now keep free products in the office.
